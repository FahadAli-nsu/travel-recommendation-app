Our story

Farflung started in 2021 as a shared notes document between three friends who kept sending each other the same question: "where should I actually go?" Two years and a few dozen countries later, that document became this site — a place for recommendations that come from having stood on the beach, climbed the temple steps, or gotten lost in the city, not from scraping someone else's blog.

Every destination on Farflung is written by someone who has been there in the last two years. No sponsored placements, no affiliate rankings — just the places we'd send a friend.

What we care about

We favor specific, useful detail over superlatives: what time to arrive, what to skip, what the weather actually does in shoulder season. We'd rather tell you a place is only worth a half-day than pad an itinerary.
